# Healing station visual style

## Fact or rule

Healing stations are tall green structures with readable world UI mounted in
their entrance. The arena should also include visual landmarks, animated light,
and responsive effects that give combat spaces a finished game feel.

## Why

The original station was a short pedestal with a floating label. The user's
earlier prompt specifying a tall green station, entrance UI, and more game feel
disappeared from the chat history and was missed.

## How to apply

Preserve the tall green portal silhouette and entrance-mounted UI whenever the
station is changed. Keep future arena art and feedback readable in a browser
build, avoid collision clutter, and keep camera shake disabled.
