# Snitch durability

## Fact or rule

A flying Snitch has 8.8 maximum health, so two ordinary 4.4-damage bullets
destroy it. Stronger ability projectiles may destroy it in one hit.

## Why

The previous 35 health made players shoot a Snitch too many times before they
could claim its ability.

## How to apply

Keep ordinary Snitch durability at two standard hits when balancing player or
projectile damage. Adjust `SNITCH_HEALTH` with `Powers.BASE_DAMAGE` if the base
damage changes later.
